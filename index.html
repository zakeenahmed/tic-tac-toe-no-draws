Sliding tic-tac-toe

X to place a mark.

How to play

  1. X and O take turns placing a mark on an empty cell. Three-in-a-row wins.
  2. Once each player has three marks, slide one of your marks one step up, down, left, or right into an empty cell.
  3. If you cannot slide, you lose. If your move repeats an earlier position, you lose. There are no draws.
